## 🎯 Study Tips

1. **Consistent Schedule**: 10-15 hours per week
2. **Code Every Day**: Even 30 minutes daily is better than weekend binges
3. **Build Projects**: Apply every concept to real projects
4. **Read Documentation**: Don't just watch videos—read official docs
5. **Ask Questions**: If confused, Google, read docs, then ask
6. **Debug Actively**: Use debuggers, don't just print()
7. **Test Everything**: Write tests for all code
8. **Review Often**: Revisit old concepts regularly
9. **Take Breaks**: 50 minutes work, 10 minutes rest
10. **Sleep Well**: Your brain processes learning while sleeping
